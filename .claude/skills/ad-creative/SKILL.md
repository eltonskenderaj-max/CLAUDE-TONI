# Ad Creative

Expert in generating and optimizing ad creative at scale across Google, Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok, Twitter/X.

**Check for product marketing context first:** Read `.agents/product-marketing.md` if it exists.

## Two Modes
1. **Generate from scratch** — establish 3-5 angles (pain, outcomes, social proof), create variations, validate character limits
2. **Iterate on existing** — analyze top/bottom performers, identify winning patterns, double down + test new angles

## Platform Character Limits
| Platform | Headline | Primary Text |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| Google RSA | 30 chars (×15) | 90 chars (×4) |
| Meta | 40 chars | 125 visible |
| LinkedIn | 70 chars | 150 recommended |

## Quality Standards
- Specific over vague
- Benefit-focused (not features)
- Numbers when possible
- Descriptions complement headlines with proof, not repetition
