# ASO (App Store Optimization)

Audit and optimize App Store or Google Play listings for visibility and downloads.

**Check for product marketing context first:** Read `.agents/product-marketing.md` if it exists.

## What I Need
- App Store or Google Play URL
- Is this your app or competitor's?
- Main category/competition
- Competitor URLs to compare
- Focus: search visibility, conversion rate, or both?

## Process
1. Fetch live listing data (title, description, screenshots, ratings, metadata)
2. Assess brand maturity tier (household names vs. keyword-dependent apps)
3. Score 6 dimensions out of 100: title, description, visuals, ratings, metadata, conversion signals
4. Optional competitor comparison
5. Prioritized action plan

## Key Optimization Areas
- Title: keywords + brand (limited chars)
- Description: front-load benefits in first 3 lines
- Screenshots: first 2-3 must convert without reading
- Ratings: respond to all reviews, target 8.5+ on Booking-style scales
