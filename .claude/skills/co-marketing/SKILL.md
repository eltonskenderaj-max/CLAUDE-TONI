# Co-Marketing

Strategy for joint campaigns with complementary partners.

**Check for product marketing context first:** Read `.agents/product-marketing.md` if it exists.

## Partner Identification
Score partners on: fit, audience size, alignment, engagement, reciprocity, execution ease.
Find partners via: integration ecosystems, community signals, shared audience analysis.

## Campaign Types
- Content partnerships (co-authored guides, joint reports)
- Webinars with shared promotion
- Product integrations
- Community initiatives
- Newsletter swaps

## Partnership Agreement Covers
- Lead ownership and sharing
- Promotion commitments
- Responsibilities and timelines
- Metrics and success criteria
- Branding and data sharing

## Key Metrics
Leads generated, conversion rates, audience growth, engagement, collaboration ease, brand lift.
