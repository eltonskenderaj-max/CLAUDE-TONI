# Cold Email

Writing cold emails that get replies. Philosophy: write like a peer, not a vendor.

**Check for product marketing context first:** Read `.agents/product-marketing.md` if it exists.

## Core Principles
1. **Ruthless brevity** — Every sentence must justify its existence
2. **Lead with their problem** — Reflect their situation back to them
3. **One clear ask** — Interest-based CTAs ("Worth a 15-min call?") outperform meeting requests
4. **Personalization that connects** — Research signal → why you're reaching out

## Structure (Observation → Problem → Proof → Ask)
- Subject: 2-4 words, lowercase, internal-looking
- Opening: specific observation about them
- Bridge: connect to problem you solve
- Proof: one-line credibility
- CTA: low-commitment ask

## What Works
- Subject lines that look like internal emails
- Short follow-ups that add something new (never "just checking in")
- Specific numbers over vague claims

## What Kills Response Rates
- "I hope this email finds you well"
- Feature dumps
- Copy that sounds like marketing
