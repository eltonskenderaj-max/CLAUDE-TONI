# Community Marketing

Build and grow communities that create member value while driving business outcomes.

**Check for product marketing context first:** Read `.agents/product-marketing.md` if it exists.

## What I Need
- Product and problem it solves
- Platform (Discord, Slack, Circle, Reddit, forum)
- Stage (pre-launch, early, scaling, established)
- Primary goal (retention, activation, word-of-mouth, support)
- Ideal member profile
- Weekly bandwidth for community management

## Core Framework
1. Community identity and positioning
2. Platform selection
3. Launch roadmap
4. Onboarding ritual for new members
5. Engagement flywheel design
6. Advocate/ambassador program

## Health Metrics
- Active member rate (posted/commented in last 30 days)
- Response time to questions
- Member-generated vs. brand-generated content ratio
- Net promoter score from community members

## Related Skills
- **social**: Content strategy for community channels
- **referrals**: Turning community into referral engine
