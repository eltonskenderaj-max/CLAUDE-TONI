# Competitor Profiling

Systematic competitive intelligence: profile competitors and produce structured analysis.

**Check for product marketing context first:** Read `.agents/product-marketing.md` if it exists.

## Process
1. Confirm competitor URLs and research depth (quick scan vs. deep profile)
2. Scrape key pages: homepage, pricing, features, about, customers, integrations, changelog
3. Extract: positioning, features, pricing, messaging
4. Pull SEO metrics: domain authority, backlinks, organic keywords, traffic estimates
5. Build profile + comparative summary

## Each Profile Covers
- At-a-glance metrics (tagline, founding, team, domain rank, traffic)
- Positioning and messaging analysis
- Product capabilities and differentiators
- Pricing tiers and billing model
- Customer social proof and review ratings
- SEO strength and content strategy
- Strengths, weaknesses, competitive implications

## Principles
- Facts over opinions — all claims traceable to sources
- Current data — profiles timestamped
- Balanced view of competitor strengths AND weaknesses
