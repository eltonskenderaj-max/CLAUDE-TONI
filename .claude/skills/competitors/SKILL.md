# Competitor & Alternative Pages

Create comparison content that ranks for competitive keywords.

**Check for product marketing context first:** Read `.agents/product-marketing.md` if it exists.

## Four Page Formats
1. **"[Competitor] alternative"** — searchers actively seeking to switch
2. **"[Competitor] alternatives"** — broader research
3. **"You vs Competitor"** — direct comparison
4. **"Competitor A vs B"** — third-party comparison introducing your product

## Page Structure
- TL;DR summary (2-3 sentences)
- At-a-glance comparison table
- Detailed breakdowns: features, pricing, support, integrations
- Honest "who each option suits best"
- Migration guidance and customer testimonials
- Clear CTAs

## Key Principle
Acknowledge competitor strengths and your limitations. Readers will verify claims — accuracy builds trust.

## Maintenance
- Quarterly: pricing changes
- Annually: full competitor reassessment
