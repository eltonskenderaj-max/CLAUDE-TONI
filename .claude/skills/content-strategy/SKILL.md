# Content Strategy Guide

## Key Principles

**Searchable vs. Shareable Content**
The framework prioritizes content as either searchable (capturing existing demand through optimization) or shareable (creating demand through novel insights). Search traffic is the foundation.

**Before Starting**
Gather context on business goals, customer research (pain points, objections, support patterns), current resources, and competitive landscape.

## Content Types

**Searchable formats** include use-case content (persona + specific scenario), hub-and-spoke structures (comprehensive overview + related subtopics), and template libraries.

**Shareable formats** encompass thought leadership, data-driven insights, expert roundups, case studies, and behind-the-scenes content revealing company decisions.

## Strategy Development

**Content Pillars** (3-5 core topics) spawn topic clusters. Most content works fine under `/blog` with strategic internal linking; dedicated URL structures are only necessary for major topics requiring layered depth.

**Keyword Research by Buyer Stage:**
- Awareness: "what is," "how to," "guide to"
- Consideration: "best," "vs," "alternatives"
- Decision: "pricing," "reviews," "demo"
- Implementation: "templates," "tutorial," "setup"

## Ideation Sources

Extract content ideas from keyword data, sales transcripts, survey responses, forum research (Reddit, Quora), competitor analysis, and support team feedback.

## Prioritization

Score ideas across: customer impact (40%), content-market fit (30%), search potential (20%), and resource requirements (10%).

The deliverable includes content pillars with rationale, prioritized topics with keywords and buyer stages, and a visual topic cluster map.
