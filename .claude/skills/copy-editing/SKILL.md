# Copy Editing

Systematic approach to improving existing marketing copy through seven sequential sweeps.

**Check for product marketing context first:** Read `.agents/product-marketing.md` if it exists.

## The Seven Sweeps
1. **Clarity** — Readers understand without confusion
2. **Voice and Tone** — Consistent brand personality
3. **So What** — Every claim answers "why should I care?"
4. **Prove It** — Assertions include supporting evidence
5. **Specificity** — Replace vague with concrete details and numbers
6. **Heightened Emotion** — Adds resonance and connection
7. **Zero Risk** — Remove barriers to action near CTAs

## Key Principle
Enhance rather than rewrite — preserve the author's original intent.

## Common Problems Fixed
- Walls of features → benefits and outcomes
- Corporate jargon → plain language
- Weak openings → hook in the first line
- Missing proof → specific social proof and data
