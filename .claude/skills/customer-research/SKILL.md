# Customer Research

Uncover what customers actually think, feel, say, and struggle with.

**Check for product marketing context first:** Read `.agents/product-marketing.md` if it exists.

## Two Modes
1. **Analyze existing assets** — interviews, surveys, reviews, support tickets, NPS
2. **Digital watering hole research** — Reddit, G2, Capterra, YouTube comments, forums

## Extraction Framework (for each asset)
1. Jobs to Be Done (functional, emotional, social)
2. Pain Points — prioritize unprompted + emotional language
3. Trigger Events — what changed that made them seek a solution?
4. Desired Outcomes — in their exact words
5. Language/Vocabulary — gold for copy
6. Alternatives Considered — competitors, DIY, do nothing

## Research Quality Labels
- **High**: 3+ independent sources, unprompted, consistent across segments
- **Medium**: 2 sources, or prompted, or one segment
- **Low**: Single source, needs validation

## Deliverable Formats
Research synthesis, VOC quote bank, persona document, JTBD map, competitive intel summary, research gap analysis.
