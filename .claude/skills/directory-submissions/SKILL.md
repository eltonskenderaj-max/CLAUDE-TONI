# Directory Submissions

Build backlinks and discovery foundation through strategic directory submissions.

**Check for product marketing context first:** Read `.agents/product-marketing.md` if it exists.

## Before Submitting
Ensure you have: pricing page, screenshots, demo video, destination pages, short/long description variants.

## Tier Strategy
- **Startup directories**: Product Hunt, Indie Hackers, BetaList
- **SaaS directories**: G2, Capterra, GetApp, AlternativeTo
- **AI/Agent directories**: Futurepedia, There's An AI For That, TopAI.tools
- **Niche directories**: Match to your specific category

## Submission Sequence (Weeks 1-3)
- Week 1: Startup + SaaS foundational directories
- Week 2: Category-specific directories
- Week 3: AI and niche directories

## Product Hunt Strategy
- 3-week warm-up: build hunter relationships, prepare assets
- Launch day: engage every comment, don't ask for upvotes — ask for feedback
- Follow-up: capture emails from traffic spike

## Key Principle
Directories feed link equity into destination pages — not standalone strategy.
