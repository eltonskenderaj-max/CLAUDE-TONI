# Email Sequence Design Guide

## Core Framework

**"One Email, One Job"** — each message should have one primary purpose with a single main call-to-action. Success relies on three principles: delivering value before asking, prioritizing relevance over volume, and creating clear paths forward.

## Sequence Types

**Welcome sequences** (5-7 emails): Build trust and activate new signups through quick wins and social proof.

**Lead nurture sequences** (6-8 emails): Establish expertise over 2-3 weeks using problem/solution frameworks and case studies.

**Re-engagement sequences** (3-4 emails): Win back inactive users with genuine check-ins and incentives.

**Onboarding sequences** (5-7 emails): Guide product users toward key activation moments.

## Timing Strategy

Immediate delivery for welcome emails, then space them 1-4 days apart depending on sequence type. B2B sequences should avoid weekends; consider local time zones for sends.

## Copy Essentials

Keep emails scannable with short paragraphs, white space, and 150-300 words for educational content. Subject lines work best when specific and benefit-driven (questions, how-tos, or direct statements perform well).

## Key Question Before Starting

Clarify the trigger event, primary goal, audience context, and what defines success for your specific sequence.
