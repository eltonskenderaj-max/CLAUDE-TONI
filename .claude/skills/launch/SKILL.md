# Launch Strategy

Product launch planning using the ORB Framework (Owned, Rented, Borrowed channels).

**Check for product marketing context first:** Read `.agents/product-marketing.md` if it exists.

## Five Launch Phases
1. **Internal** — Test with friendly users
2. **Alpha** — External validation, early access
3. **Beta** — Scale testing, buzz-building
4. **Early Access** — Controlled expansion
5. **Full Launch** — Open availability

## Channel Strategy
- **Owned** (email, blog, community): compounds in value, algorithm-independent
- **Rented** (social, marketplaces): speed but funnel users to owned assets
- **Borrowed** (guest posts, influencers): credibility shortcuts

## Product Hunt
- 3 weeks before: build hunter relationships, prepare assets
- Launch day: engage every comment, ask for feedback not upvotes
- Convert traffic to owned assets (email list)

## Core Principle
"The best companies don't just launch once — they launch again and again."
