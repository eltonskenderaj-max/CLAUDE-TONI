# Lead Magnets

Plan lead magnets that capture emails, generate qualified leads, and lead to product adoption.

**Check for product marketing context first:** Read `.agents/product-marketing.md` if it exists.

## Lead Magnet Types by Effort
| Type | Effort | Timeline |
|------|--------|----------|
| Checklist | Low | 1-2 hours |
| Template | Low-Med | 2-8 hours |
| Ebook/guide | High | 1-3 weeks |
| Mini-course (email) | Medium | 1-2 weeks |
| Quiz/assessment | Medium | 1-2 weeks |

## Match to Buyer Stage
- **Awareness**: Checklist, ebook — educate on problem
- **Consideration**: Assessment, comparison template — help evaluate
- **Decision**: Implementation template, ROI calculator — remove friction

## Gating Strategy
- Email only: highest conversion, lowest friction
- Each extra field reduces conversion 5-10%
- Partial gate: preview + full version for balance

## Landing Page Structure
Headline → Preview/mockup → "What's inside" bullets → Social proof → Minimal form → FAQ
