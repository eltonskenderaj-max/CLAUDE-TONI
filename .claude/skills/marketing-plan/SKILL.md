# Marketing Plan Skill

This is a comprehensive marketing strategy framework for fractional CMOs and consultants.

## Core Purpose

Generates a 13-section, AARRR-structured (Acquisition, Activation, Retention, Referral, Revenue) marketing plan tailored to a client's actual budget, team size, and growth stage.

## When to Use

- Building a 12-month marketing roadmap for a new client engagement
- Consolidating scattered marketing work into one cohesive strategy
- Creating investor-ready marketing documentation
- Sequencing audit findings into executable priorities

**Avoid using for:** Single-channel tactics (use channel-specific skills instead) or generic idea generation.

## The 13-Section Structure

1. **Executive summary** — Three major bets and 90-day priorities
2. **Strategic frame** — Category positioning, ICP, brand voice
3. **Current state** — Team, budget, status quo assessment (scored against 17-section rubric)
4. **Acquisition through Revenue** — Five funnel stages with specific tactics
5. **90-day roadmap** — Week-by-week phased execution with owners
6. **12-month outlook** — Quarterly milestones tied to funding events
7. **Marketing operations stack** — Skills and tool integrations per stage
8. **Tactical idea bank** — 139 cross-referenced ideas with status (Now/Q2/Skip)
9. **Measurement and RACI** — Metrics, team accountability, blocking decisions

## Key Customization Points

Every plan must address:
- Exact current budget ($X/month by category)
- Unit economics (ARPC, retention rate, blended CAC)
- Team roles and skill gaps
- Current channel performance
- Growth phase ($0–10K, $10K–100K, $100K–1M, or $1M+ ARR)
- Funding milestones and capability unlocks

## Quality Signal

Strong plans name specific AARRR stages for each move, avoid recommending beyond current team capacity, and explicitly list what's *not* being done and why.
