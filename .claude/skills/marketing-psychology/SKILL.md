# Marketing Psychology & Mental Models

Apply psychological principles to marketing decisions.

**Check for product marketing context first:** Read `.agents/product-marketing.md` if it exists.

## Five Categories

### 1. Foundational Thinking Models
First Principles, Jobs to Be Done, Circle of Competence, Inversion — for strategic decisions.

### 2. Understanding Buyers
Confirmation bias, availability heuristic, loss aversion — how customers make decisions.

### 3. Influencing Behavior
- **Reciprocity**: Give first (free tools, content)
- **Authority**: Credentials, expertise signals
- **Social proof**: Numbers, testimonials, logos
- **Scarcity/Urgency**: Real limits, not manufactured

### 4. Pricing Psychology
- Charm pricing ($97 vs $100)
- Anchoring (show higher price first)
- Mental accounting (investment vs. expense framing)
- Decoy pricing (make middle tier look best)

### 5. Design & Delivery
AIDA funnel, BJ Fogg Behavior Model (Motivation × Ability × Trigger), Nudge Theory.

## Key Principle
Test assumptions with real customers before implementing psychological tactics.
