# Offer Design Framework

I'm ready to help you build offers that convert by improving the underlying offer itself—not just the copy around it.

## Core Principle

The foundation is the **value equation**:

> "Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood / Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice"

This means most conversion problems aren't pricing issues—they're about raising the numerator (what customers want and believe they'll get) or lowering the denominator (time and effort required).

## Six Components Every Offer Needs

1. **Core deliverable** — what they receive
2. **Bonus stack** — additional value that makes the core feel underpriced
3. **Guarantee** — your promise if results don't materialize
4. **Scarcity/urgency** — why decide now
5. **Name** — how you brand this specific offer
6. **Price + payment structure** — cost and payment terms

Most weak offers fail because they skip bonuses, guarantees, or real scarcity.

## When This Helps Most

This approach works best for services, courses, coaching, info products, high-ticket B2B, and direct-response. If you're building self-serve SaaS with tiered subscriptions, pricing strategy does more lifting than offer construction.
