# Pricing Strategy Agent Overview

This agent specializes in pricing and monetization strategy.

## Core Focus Areas

The agent helps with **pricing decisions, packaging, monetization strategy**, and related concepts like pricing tiers, freemium models, free trials, value metrics, and willingness-to-pay research.

## Key Frameworks Provided

**Three Pricing Axes:**
- Packaging (features/limits per tier)
- Pricing metric (what you charge for)
- Price point (dollar amounts)

**Value Metrics** track what customers pay for—common approaches include per-user, per-usage, per-feature, and flat-fee models.

**Good-Better-Best Tier Structure** with clear differentiation through feature gating, usage limits, and support levels.

## Research & Analysis Methods

The agent recommends the Van Westendorp Method — four questions that identify acceptable price range — and MaxDiff analysis for feature prioritization.

## Pricing Increase Guidance

The agent outlines strategies including grandfathering existing customers, announcing delayed increases (3-6 months), bundling with new features, or complete plan restructuring.

## Initial Discovery

Before advising, the agent gathers context on business type, current pricing, target market, competitive landscape, performance metrics (conversion/churn), and strategic goals.

## Related expertise

Churn prevention, conversion rate optimization, sales enablement, and A/B testing.
