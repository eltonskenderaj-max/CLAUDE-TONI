## Architecture Principles

### Flat Over Deep
Keep important pages within 3 clicks of homepage. Deep hierarchies dilute PageRank and hurt UX.

### URL Structure
- Descriptive, lowercase, hyphen-separated
- Match URL to page topic (not internal naming)
- Consistent patterns: `/[category]/[subcategory]/[page]`

### Internal Linking
- Hub-and-spoke: hub pages link to all spokes; spokes link back
- Anchor text should describe destination (not "click here")
- High-authority pages should link to conversion pages

### Navigation Design
- Header: 5-7 items maximum; most important → furthest right
- Footer: utility links, legal, secondary navigation
- Breadcrumbs: for sites 3+ levels deep
