## Compliance Fundamentals

### US (TCPA + A2P 10DLC)
- Written consent required before sending marketing texts
- HELP and STOP keywords must be supported
- Register brand and campaigns via A2P 10DLC (required for all US business texting)
- Quiet hours: avoid 8pm–8am local time

### EU (GDPR)
- Explicit opt-in required
- Easy unsubscribe in every message
- Data minimization principles

## Copy Guidelines

- 160 characters = 1 SMS segment (keep under to avoid splitting)
- Always include: who you are, the offer/message, and a link or CTA
- Personalization lifts CTR significantly (first name at minimum)
- No URL shorteners that hide destination (use branded short links)

## Key Metrics

- Opt-in rate
- Click-through rate (CTR)
- Revenue per send
- Unsubscribe rate (red flag if >2%)
- List growth rate

## High-Value Flows (by priority)

2. Abandoned cart (1, 4, 24 hours)
3. Post-purchase (confirmation + review request)
4. Win-back (lapsed customers at 60/90 days)
5. VIP / loyalty tiers
