The problem

Why hotels lose between 12% and 25% of revenue?

Most independent hotels in Albania leave a significant portion of their potential revenue unrealised. Here is why:

🎲

Prices set by intuition

Without data analysis or professional tools, rates are decided by gut feeling, missing revenue opportunities during high-demand periods.

📱

Excessive OTA dependency

Booking.com, Expedia and other OTAs charging 15% to 25% commission drastically reduce your margin on every reservation.

📊

Lack of daily analysis

Without daily monitoring of performance data, competitor rates and demand signals, it is impossible to adjust strategy at the right moment.

😰

Prices too low out of fear of vacancies

The fear of empty rooms drives hoteliers to slash prices, reducing revenue even when demand is sufficient to justify higher rates.

📉

Under-exploited peak periods

Peak demand periods (summer, holidays, events) are under-capitalised with prices that do not reflect the real value of rooms at those key moments.

🔮

Inaccurate forecasting

Without structured forecasting methodology, it is impossible to anticipate staffing needs, negotiate with partners or plan investments.

⏰

Late or unstructured decisions

Price adjustments made too late or without methodology lead to cycles of over- and under-pricing that weaken long-term performance.

12% – 25%

That is the share of revenue your hotel loses every year without a professional Revenue Management strategy. Our mission: recover this revenue for you.
